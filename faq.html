Frequently Asked Questions

  • How much does it cost to join?

    • The fee is $x/month, but you can join a trip as a one-off for +-$160.

  • How many people are in the GTSSC?

    • Currently, there is a team of 7 in charge of the club. At events, there can be anywhere from 10 to 18 or more.

  • How do we get to the ranges?

    • Participants can choose to bring their own vehicle or carpool with other members.

  • I don't have a PAL (Possession and Acquisition Licence), can I still participate in shoots?

    • Absolutely, a PAL is not required to shoot.

  • Do I need to have completed my basic training / trade course to join?

    • Not at all. Even if you've never touched a firearm before, we have skilled and experienced members to help out.
