The Garrison Toronto Sports Shooting Club

The Garrison Toronto Sports Shooting Club (GTSSC) offers CAF members an opportunity to enjoy a day on the range without the responsibility that comes with being on course or exercise. In association with the PSP, the GTSSC plans both day trips and overnight stays at a multitude of ranges, including areas like Winona and Burwash.

PHOTO

Range Days

GTSSC events can either be overnight or single-day trips.

Overnights

Overnight trips start in the morning at Denison Armoury, finishing packing the vehicle(s), and starting on the long drive to the range. Once there, the vehicles are unloaded into a troop shelter or straight onto the range, and then the shooting begins. Once the sun starts to set and/or the group starts to tire, a barbequeue is lit and everyone relaxes around the fire before heading to sleep. In the morning, everything is loaded up again and, if there's enough time, there's one final shoot before heading back to the armoury.
